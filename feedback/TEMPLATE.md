# Feedback

Drop notes here anytime — between sessions, after a session, or as soon as something comes to mind. Your tutor reads this folder at the start of every session and adjusts.

**File naming:** `YYYY-MM-DD-short-title.md` (e.g., `2026-04-15-pacing-too-slow.md`). One topic per file. Delete a file once the tutor has addressed it (or let them delete it for you).

**What to write about:**

- **Pacing** — too fast, too slow, too much homework, not enough challenge
- **What's working** — an activity, explanation, or approach that clicked. Tell your tutor so they do more of it.
- **What's not working** — a topic you keep missing, an explanation that confused you, a tool you can't get into the habit of using
- **Emotional state** — burnt out, frustrated, bored, excited, confident. Your tutor can't see your face; telling them matters.
- **Real-world encounters** — "I tried to order coffee in Spanish and froze" is gold. So is "a coworker complimented my accent."
- **Goal changes** — if your reason for learning Spanish has shifted, say so. The tutor will re-plan.
- **Bugs / weirdness** — if the tutor does something confusing, contradicts itself, or gets a fact wrong, report it here.

**What NOT to put here:**

- Specific Spanish questions (those go in `parking-lot.md`)
- Homework answers or journal entries (those have their own folders)

**Template:**

```markdown
---
date: 2026-04-15
topic: pacing
---

Session today felt rushed — you introduced the preterite and moved straight into practice
without enough modeling first. Next time, can we slow down on new grammar?
```

Don't overthink it. Even a one-sentence note is useful.
