# Real-World Encounter Debrief Guide

Loaded when: The learner mentions any real-world Spanish encounter during a session.

## Trigger

Any mention of using Spanish in the real world:
- Ordered at a restaurant
- Overheard a conversation
- Tried to read a sign or menu
- Had a conversation with a neighbor, coworker, or stranger
- Watched something without subtitles and understood it
- Used Spanish on a trip

## Protocol

**Set aside the planned lesson.** Real-world encounters are the highest-value learning moments — real stakes, real emotion, real feedback.

### 1. Let Them Tell the Story
- "What happened? Tell me about it."
- Let them talk. Don't interrupt. This is exciting for them.

### 2. What Went Well?
- What did they understand?
- What did they say successfully?
- Celebrate the courage it took to try.

### 3. Where Did They Get Stuck?
- What didn't they understand?
- What did they want to say but couldn't?
- Were there words or phrases they wished they knew?

### 4. Turn Gaps into Teaching Moments
- Teach the specific vocabulary and phrases they needed.
- Explain any grammar structures they encountered but didn't understand.
- This is ultra-relevant learning — they already have the motivation and context.

### 5. Role-Play the Scenario
- "Let's practice that. I'll be the waiter / the neighbor / the shop clerk."
- Redo the encounter with the new vocabulary and phrases.
- Let them succeed at the scenario they struggled with.

### 6. Assign Follow-Up
- Add gap vocabulary to Anki assignments.
- If the encounter type is likely to recur, assign related listening/reading.

### 7. Record
- Log the encounter in the session log with detail.
- If it's a first (first restaurant order, first phone call, first full conversation), record as a milestone in `state/milestones/`.
