# Session Variety Guide

Loaded when: Tutor selects an alternative session format based on triggers below.

**The decision engine selects concepts; this guide selects format.** Run concept selection normally via the decision engine, then apply the alternative format to those concepts instead of the standard 4-phase routine.

## Selection Table

| Format | Trigger | Frequency | Phase |
|--------|---------|-----------|-------|
| Game Day | Motivation dipping, energy low, or 2 weeks since last game session | 1x every 2 weeks max | A+ |
| Story Building | 2+ grammar concepts in consolidation, especially past tenses or connectors | 1x every 2 weeks | B+ |
| Media Reaction | Weekly narrow topic has good media available, or learner needs listening variety | 1x per week max | B+ |
| Teach-Back | Concept appears acquired but unscaffolded performance is untested or uncertain | As needed | B+ |
| Immersion-Only | Phase transition approaching, or learner confidence needs a push | 1x every 2 weeks | C+ |
| Real-World Simulation | Sprint mode active, upcoming real-world need, or learner requests scenario practice | As needed during sprint; 1x/month otherwise | B+ |

## Formats

### Game Day

**When to use:** Motivation is dipping, learner reports low energy, or it has been 2+ weeks since a game session.

**How to run:**
1. Pick 2-3 games based on today's focus concepts.
2. Explain rules briefly. Keep explanations in the target language ratio for the current phase.
3. Play each game for 5-8 minutes. Rotate before energy drops.
4. Brief review at the end: corrections batched, highlight clever moments.

**Games:** 20 questions in Spanish, word association chains, "describe and guess" (describe a concept without using the word itself), translation race (rapid English-to-Spanish, track speed improvement over sessions).

**What to assess:** Vocabulary recall speed, risk-taking willingness, circumlocution ability, which word categories cause hesitation.

### Story Building

**When to use:** Grammar consolidation phase, especially when past tenses, connectors, or sequencing words need integration practice.

**How to run:**
1. Tutor sets the scene with 1-2 sentences establishing a character and situation.
2. Learner adds 1-2 sentences continuing the story.
3. Alternate back and forth. Tutor steers toward structures that need practice.
4. After 10-15 exchanges, wrap up the story together.
5. Review: reread the story, note 2-3 corrections, highlight creative use of target structures.

**Tutor technique:** Model target structures in your turns. If the learner avoids past tense, introduce a time jump ("Al dia siguiente...") that forces it. **Assess:** Spontaneous use of target grammar, narrative coherence, connector variety, self-correction.

### Media Reaction

**When to use:** Need listening variety, weekly topic has good short-form media, or learner needs exposure to natural speech patterns.

**How to run:**
1. Select a 3-5 minute video clip appropriate for the learner's level. Prefer content related to the weekly narrow topic.
2. First watch: learner summarizes what they understood (no pressure for completeness).
3. Discuss: 3-4 comprehension questions, then open-ended reaction questions.
4. Second watch (optional): listen for specific details or structures from today's focus.
5. Free discussion about the content in Spanish.

**Sources:** Dreaming Spanish, Easy Spanish (YouTube), short news clips, curated podcast segments. Log in `resource-tracker.yaml`. **Assess:** Listening comprehension depth, spontaneous production quality, opinion expression, vocabulary gaps.

### Teach-Back

**When to use:** Concept appears acquired but you want to verify depth, or `performance_scaffolded` is strong but `performance_unscaffolded` is untested.

**How to run:**
1. "Explain [concept] to me as if I were a beginner learning Spanish."
2. Let them explain without interruption. Note gaps and misconceptions.
3. Ask 2-3 probing questions targeting edge cases or common exceptions.
4. Gently correct misconceptions. Reframe if their mental model is off.
5. Test with 2-3 novel examples that require the concept but were not practiced before.

**What to assess:** Can they articulate the rule (not just apply it), awareness of exceptions, novel example generation, confidence during explanation.

### Immersion-Only

**When to use:** Phase transition from B to C or C to D is approaching, or learner needs a confidence boost.

**How to run:**
1. Greet in Spanish. Set the expectation: "Hoy, todo en espanol."
2. Learner may use communication repair phrases (taught by session 5) but no English.
3. If stuck, offer choices rather than switching to English: "Quieres decir X o Y?"
4. Run a lighter version of the standard session. Fewer new concepts, more conversation.
5. Debrief at the end (may switch to English): how did it feel, what was hardest, what surprised them.

**What to assess:** Sustained production ability, repair strategy use, confidence trajectory, which domains force English fallback.

### Real-World Simulation

**When to use:** Sprint mode active, upcoming real-world need, or learner requests scenario practice. More structured than standard conversation practice role-play.

**How to run:**
1. Define the scenario: setting, learner's role, tutor's role, goal to accomplish.
2. Rehearse key vocabulary and phrases for 3-5 minutes before starting.
3. Run the simulation for 8-12 minutes. Play the role realistically, including unexpected complications.
4. Pause and reset if stuck. Provide the phrase they needed, then resume.
5. Run it a second time. The delta between attempts is the real assessment.
6. Debrief: what went well, what phrases to drill, assign targeted homework.

**Scenarios:** Airport/travel, medical (describing symptoms), professional (interview, presentation), social (introductions, ordering food), emergency (reporting a problem).

**What to assess:** Task completion, recovery from unexpected turns, appropriate register, key phrase automaticity. Log scenario outcomes in the session log.
