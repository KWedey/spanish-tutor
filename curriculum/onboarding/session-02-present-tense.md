# Session 2: Present Tense Regular Verbs

## Concept
A-01 Present Tense Regular (-ar, -er, -ir)

## Prerequisites Check
- A-00 repair phrases should be memorized (can produce without hesitation)
- Anki is installed and the learner completed at least one review session

## Session Plan

### Review (5-8 min)
**Repair phrase drill:** "If I said something you didn't understand right now, what would you say?" Test 3-4 repair phrases in rapid succession.

**Anki verification:** "How did Anki go? Any cards that felt tricky?" Verify the learner actually used it (ask specifics: "Which cards kept coming back?").

**Quick greeting exchange:** Open with a Spanish greeting. Does the learner respond naturally?

### New Material (15-20 min)
Load `curriculum/grammar/A-foundation/01-present-regular.md`.

**Stage 1 — Noticing (5 min):**
Show 6 sentences using "hablar" with different subjects. Ask: "What pattern do you notice about the endings?" Let the learner discover the conjugation pattern before explaining it.

**Stage 2 — Explicit instruction (5 min):**
Explain the three verb groups (-ar, -er, -ir). Show full paradigm for hablar, comer, vivir. Emphasize that endings carry the subject — "In Spanish, 'hablo' already means 'I speak.' You don't need 'yo.'"

**L1 interference preemption:** "Your English brain will want to always say 'yo hablo,' 'tu hablas.' Native speakers drop the pronoun most of the time. If you hear yourself saying 'yo' before every verb, that's your English habit showing."

**Stage 3 — Controlled practice (5 min):**
Conjugation drill: tutor gives infinitive + subject, learner conjugates. Start with -ar only, then add -er, then -ir. 8-10 items.

Introduce daily routine verbs during the drill: trabajar, comer, vivir, estudiar, cocinar, hablar, leer, escribir.

### Practice (5-10 min)
Guided conversation: "Tell me about your typical day. What do you do in the morning? The afternoon? The evening?"

Scaffolding: provide verb prompts if the learner stalls ("What about eating? Cooking? Studying?"). Note which verbs they attempt and which they avoid. Listen for subject pronoun overuse.

### Checkout (3-5 min)
**Anki:** Add 8 new cards — high-frequency verbs: trabajar, comer, vivir, estudiar, cocinar, hablar, leer, escribir (infinitive on front, meaning + example conjugation on back).

**Tool setup: SpanishDict**
- Show the learner SpanishDict.com — bookmark it as their primary dictionary
- Demonstrate the conjugation tool: type any verb, see all conjugations
- "When you're doing homework and you're not sure about a verb, look it up here"

**Homework (20-25 min total):**
- Anki review — all cards (10 min)
- Write 5 sentences about your daily routine using different verbs (10 min)
- Optional: Watch one Dreaming Spanish superbeginner video (10 min) — preview for session 3 tool setup

## Vocabulary Focus
Continue `curriculum/vocabulary/tier1-survival/greetings-introductions.md` — add daily routine verbs: trabajar, comer, vivir, estudiar, cocinar, hablar, leer, escribir. These double as grammar practice for the present tense paradigm.

## Learner Observation Targets
- **Learning speed:** How quickly do they internalize the conjugation pattern? Do they need the paradigm table visible, or can they produce from memory after a few drills?
- **Grammar preference:** Do they want rules explained first, or do they prefer to work from examples?
- **Subject pronoun overuse:** How frequently do they include unnecessary yo/tu/el? This is the primary L1 marker to track.
- **Verb avoidance:** Are they sticking to -ar verbs and avoiding -er/-ir? If so, push -er/-ir in drills.

## Adjustment Notes
- If repair phrases are NOT solid: spend an extra 3-5 min drilling them before starting new material. Repair phrases must be automatic by session 5.
- If the learner grasps conjugation immediately: skip some Stage 2 drills and move to Stage 3 (guided production) earlier. Add more complex sentences ("My brother works and studies").
- If the learner struggles with the pattern: slow down. Focus on -ar only this session. Defer -er/-ir to session 3 warm-up. The pattern matters more than covering all three groups today.
