# Session 1: Discovery and Repair Phrases

## Concept
A-00 Communication Repair Phrases (memorized chunks, not grammar)

## Prerequisites Check
None — this is the first session. The tutor also loads `curriculum/tutor-guides/first-session.md` for the discovery protocol.

## Session Plan

### Review (0 min)
Not applicable — first session.

### New Material (20-25 min)

**Part 1 — Discovery protocol (10-12 min):**
Follow `curriculum/tutor-guides/first-session.md`. Gather:
- Goals and motivations for learning Spanish
- Any prior Spanish experience (classes, travel, family)
- Available study time per day/week
- Target dialect preference (default: Mexican Spanish)
- Preferred learning style (rules-first vs examples-first)
- Correction preference (immediate vs batched)

**Part 2 — Repair phrases (10-12 min):**
Load `curriculum/grammar/A-foundation/00-communication-repair.md`.

Introduce 6 core phrases as memorized chunks — do NOT explain grammar:
1. "No entiendo" (I don't understand)
2. "Mas despacio, por favor" (More slowly, please)
3. "¿Puedes repetir, por favor?" (Can you repeat?)
4. "¿Que significa [word]?" (What does [word] mean?)
5. "¿Como se dice [English word]?" (How do you say [word]?)
6. "¿Puedes hablar mas lento?" (Can you speak slower?)

Teaching method: Say each phrase, have learner repeat 2-3 times. After all 6, do rapid-fire drills: tutor describes a scenario ("You didn't catch what I said"), learner responds with the appropriate phrase.

### Practice (5-8 min)
Role-play stuck scenarios. Tutor speaks Spanish at natural speed — learner must use repair phrases to navigate:
- Tutor says something too fast — learner asks to slow down
- Tutor uses an unknown word — learner asks what it means
- Tutor says something the learner missed — learner asks for repetition

Introduce 5 basic greetings during practice: hola, buenos dias, adios, gracias, por favor. Use them naturally as part of the role-play.

### Checkout (3-5 min)
**Tool setup: Anki**
- Help the learner install Anki (desktop or mobile)
- Create a "Spanish" deck
- Add first 10 cards: 6 repair phrases + 4 greetings (hola, buenos dias, adios, gracias)

**Homework (15-20 min total):**
- Memorize repair phrases — practice saying them aloud (5 min)
- Complete first Anki session — review all 10 cards (5 min)
- Listen to the repair phrases again and repeat after each one (5 min)

## Vocabulary Focus
Begin `curriculum/vocabulary/tier1-survival/greetings-introductions.md` — introduce only: hola, buenos dias, buenos tardes, buenos noches, adios, gracias, por favor. Full cluster work starts session 2.

## Learner Observation Targets
- **Prior knowledge:** How much Spanish do they already know? Any words or phrases?
- **Energy and engagement:** Excited, nervous, cautious? Match your tone accordingly.
- **Tech comfort:** How easily do they navigate Anki setup?
- **Correction response:** When correcting pronunciation of repair phrases, do they seem to appreciate it or tense up?
- **Learning pace:** How many repetitions before a phrase sticks?

## Adjustment Notes
- If the learner has significant prior Spanish: spend less time on repair phrases (they may already know them). Use extra time for a longer discovery conversation to map existing knowledge.
- If the learner is overwhelmed by Anki setup: walk through it step-by-step. If tech is a real barrier, defer setup to session 2 and assign phrase memorization only.
- If the learner breezes through all 6 phrases in minutes: introduce 2-3 circumlocution strategies from A-00 ("Es como...", "Es una cosa que...") as a stretch goal.
