# Session 10: Final Consolidation and Transition

## Concept
Final Consolidation — no new grammar. All-concept review, skill-map population, and transition to adaptive sessions.

## Prerequisites Check
- All 8 Phase A concepts introduced (A-00 through A-07)
- Sessions 2-9 completed
- Repair phrases must be automatic
- All tools set up: Anki, SpanishDict, Dreaming Spanish, Language Transfer, Speechling

## Session Plan

### Review (5-8 min)
**Comprehensive homework check:** Review session 9 homework — irregular verb sentences. Check for regularization errors and saber/conocer usage.

**Tool engagement summary:** Briefly review all tool usage patterns across onboarding. Which tools has the learner used consistently? Which have they dropped? "Which of the tools we've set up do you find most useful? Least useful?"

**Energy and time check:** "This is our last structured session. After today, I'll tailor every session to what you need most. How are you feeling? How much time do we have?"

### Consolidation (20-25 min)
Use `curriculum/activities/grammar-in-context.md` for integration testing across ALL Phase A concepts.

**Round 1 — Foundation concepts (7 min):**
"Tell me about a typical day at work or school. Describe the place, the people, and what everyone does."
- Tests: A-01 (present regular), A-03 (gender/agreement), A-04 (articles/prepositions), A-02 (ser for descriptions, estar for locations)
- Listen for: subject pronoun overuse, adjective placement, ser/estar selection, preposition accuracy

**Round 2 — Questions and gustar (7 min):**
Two-part activity:
1. Learner interviews the tutor: "Ask me 5 questions about my favorite things." Tests A-05 (question formation) + A-06 (gustar in questions: "Te gusta...?")
2. Tutor asks the learner negative questions: "Don't you like...? Do you never...?" Learner responds with double negatives. Tests A-05 (negation).

**Round 3 — Irregular verbs in context (6 min):**
"What do you want to do on your next vacation? Where do you want to go? What can you do there? What do you know about that place?"
- Tests: A-07 (querer, ir, poder, saber/conocer, hacer) in free production
- Listen for: regularization errors, verb avoidance, saber vs conocer distinction

**Assessment notes:** During all three rounds, note the status of each concept:
- **Approaching acquired:** Produces correctly in unscaffolded conversation without self-correction pauses
- **Practicing:** Produces correctly most of the time but still makes errors or pauses to think
- **Introduced:** Can produce with scaffolding but struggles in free conversation

Record observations per concept for the first skill-map context performance population.

### Practice (5-10 min)
**Extended free conversation:** Choose a topic that the learner cares about — something from their goals, interests, or recent experiences. Let them talk for 3-5 minutes with minimal interruption. This is the most natural sample of their current ability.

After: 2-3 corrections on the most impactful patterns. Then: specific, genuine feedback on what has improved most since session 1. "In session 2, you were putting 'yo' before every verb. Now you drop it naturally. That's real progress."

### Transition (5 min)
**Learner profile finalization:** Confirm or update:
- Learning style preferences observed across 10 sessions
- Correction response patterns (do they prefer recasting or explicit correction?)
- Energy patterns (morning vs evening, high-energy vs reflective)
- Pacing preference (push hard vs steady progression)

**Preview adaptive sessions:** "Starting next session, I won't follow a set plan. I'll choose what to focus on based on what you need most — sometimes that's a new concept, sometimes it's practice on something we've already covered. If you ever want to work on something specific, just tell me."

**Onboarding summary:** Briefly summarize what the learner has learned:
- 7 grammar concepts (present regular, ser/estar, gender agreement, articles/prepositions, questions/negation, gustar, irregular verbs)
- Communication repair phrases
- Tier-1 survival vocabulary
- 5 learning tools

### Checkout (5 min)
**Set `onboarding_complete: true`** in the session state update.

**Populate skill-map context performance fields** for all Phase A concepts for the first time, based on today's observations.

**Journal assignment:** If the writing track has not started yet, assign the first journal entry: "Write 3-5 sentences in Spanish about anything — your day, your weekend plans, something you like. Don't worry about perfection. Just write."

**Full resource rotation homework (30 min total):**
- Anki review — all cards (10 min)
- Speechling: record 3 sentences using different grammar concepts (5-10 min)
- Dreaming Spanish: 15 min at current level (10-15 min)
- Language Transfer: 1 episode (15 min)
- Journal entry: 3-5 sentences (10 min)
- Optional: graded reader — continue or start chapter 1

**Motivational close:** Reference something specific from the learner's journey. Genuine, not generic. "You started 10 sessions ago not knowing how to say 'I like coffee' in Spanish. Now you can describe your life, ask questions, talk about what you like and what you want to do. That's real."

## Vocabulary Focus
Full review across all tier-1 clusters: `curriculum/vocabulary/tier1-survival/greetings-introductions.md`, `curriculum/vocabulary/tier1-survival/numbers-time-dates.md`, `curriculum/vocabulary/tier1-survival/basic-descriptions.md`, `curriculum/vocabulary/tier1-survival/directions-transportation.md`, `curriculum/vocabulary/tier1-survival/food-restaurant.md`. No new vocabulary introduced — assess breadth and retention across all clusters.

## Learner Observation Targets
- **Per-concept status:** This is the definitive baseline for all Phase A concepts. For each one (A-00 through A-07), note: status (introduced/practicing/approaching acquired), performance_scaffolded, performance_unscaffolded, integration_tested.
- **Strongest and weakest concepts:** Which concepts does the learner use confidently? Which require visible effort? This drives the decision engine priority for the first adaptive session.
- **Readiness for Phase B:** Are any Phase A concepts approaching "acquired"? Are prerequisites for Phase B concepts (A-01, A-02, A-04) solidifying?
- **Learner autonomy:** Does the learner self-correct? Do they ask questions about patterns they notice? Do they push themselves to use new vocabulary? Autonomy signals readiness for reduced scaffolding.
- **Overall trajectory:** Based on 10 sessions, estimate the learner's trajectory. Are they on pace for the goals set in session 1? Adjust schedule.yaml accordingly.

## Adjustment Notes
- If one or more concepts from sessions 7-9 are still at "introduced" (barely practiced): extend the consolidation rounds to focus on those. The transition preview can be briefer — the learner doesn't need a long speech about what's coming next.
- If the learner is performing well across all concepts: use this session to push the boundaries. Try more complex sentences, faster-paced conversation, or a mini role-play. The stronger the learner exits onboarding, the faster Phase B concepts can begin.
- If the learner seems anxious about losing the structured format: reassure them. "The structure doesn't disappear — I still plan every session. It's just tailored to you now instead of following a fixed order."
- If time permits and the learner is energized: do a brief role-play from `curriculum/activities/role-play-scenarios.md` — ordering at a restaurant combines food vocabulary, gustar, questions, and irregular verbs. This is a strong capstone activity.
