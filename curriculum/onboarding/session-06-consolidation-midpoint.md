# Session 6: Midpoint Consolidation

## Concept
Consolidation — no new grammar. Review of A-01, A-02, A-03, A-04 through integrated practice.

## Prerequisites Check
- Sessions 2-5 completed (A-01 present regular, A-03 gender/agreement, A-04 articles/prepositions, A-02 ser/estar)
- Repair phrases should be automatic by now — verify early in session
- All tools functional: Anki, SpanishDict, Dreaming Spanish, Language Transfer assigned

## Session Plan

### Review (8-10 min)
**Comprehensive homework check covering sessions 2-5:**
- Session 2: Ask about daily routine ("What do you do in the morning?") — tests A-01
- Session 3: Ask to describe a family member — tests A-03 agreement
- Session 4: Ask where things are ("Where is your favorite restaurant?") — tests A-04 prepositions
- Session 5: Ask about identity AND state ("Tell me who you are and how you're feeling") — tests A-02

**Tool engagement check:** How consistently has the learner used Anki? Dreaming Spanish? Language Transfer? Note engagement levels for resource-tracker.

**Repair phrase verification:** At some point, speak a full sentence at natural speed. Does the learner use a repair phrase without prompting? If yes, mark as automatic.

### Consolidation (15-20 min)
Use `curriculum/activities/grammar-in-context.md` to test concept combinations.

**Round 1 — A-01 + A-03 (5 min):**
"Describe your typical weekday. What do different family members do?" Forces present tense verbs with gender-appropriate possessives and adjective agreement. Note: accuracy on verb conjugation, gender agreement on adjectives, adjective placement.

**Round 2 — A-02 + A-04 (5 min):**
"Describe your home. Where is it? What is it like? How do you feel there?" Forces ser (characteristics) vs estar (location, states) with articles and prepositions. Note: ser/estar selection, contraction use (al, del), preposition accuracy.

**Round 3 — All four concepts combined (5 min):**
"Tell me about your best friend — who they are, what they're like, what they do, and where they live." Forces all four concepts simultaneously. This is the integration test. Note which concepts hold up under load and which degrade.

**Collect baseline error rates:** Track attempts and errors per concept during the rounds above. These become the first data points for the decision engine.

### Practice (5-10 min)
**Open conversation:** "Tell me about your family, your home, what you do, and how you're feeling today."

No scaffolding. Let the learner produce freely. This is the primary assessment of unscaffolded performance across all introduced concepts. Note errors silently.

After: 2-3 corrections on the most impactful patterns. Highlight 1-2 things that have genuinely improved since session 2.

### Checkout (3-5 min)
**Reduced homework** — this is consolidation, not expansion:
- Anki review — all cards (10 min)
- Dreaming Spanish: 10-15 min superbeginner content (10-15 min)
- No new writing assignment. If the learner wants more: suggest rewatching a Dreaming Spanish video and noting 3 words they recognized.

## Vocabulary Focus
Review all tier-1 vocabulary introduced so far: `curriculum/vocabulary/tier1-survival/greetings-introductions.md`, `curriculum/vocabulary/tier1-survival/basic-descriptions.md`, `curriculum/vocabulary/tier1-survival/numbers-time-dates.md`. No new cluster introduced this session. Focus on reinforcing existing words through the consolidation conversations.

## Learner Observation Targets
- **Per-concept error rates:** This session establishes the first baseline. Track errors for A-01, A-02, A-03, A-04 separately during the grammar-in-context rounds.
- **Integration decay:** Which concepts degrade when combined with others? A concept that is strong in isolation but weak in combination needs more integration practice.
- **Learning speed pattern:** After 5 sessions, is the learner absorbing new concepts quickly or needing extended consolidation? This informs pacing for sessions 7-10.
- **Energy and motivation:** How does the learner feel about their progress? Do they feel momentum or frustration? Calibrate the motivational close accordingly.
- **Resource engagement:** Which tools are they actually using? Which have they dropped? Update resource-tracker.

## Adjustment Notes
- If the learner has been consolidating already (due to pacing adjustments in prior sessions) and all four concepts are at "practicing" with decent accuracy: introduce A-05 (questions and negation) instead of running a full consolidation session. Use session-07 plan for the new material portion.
- If one concept is significantly weaker than the others: spend the majority of the consolidation time on that concept. Assign targeted homework for that concept specifically.
- If the learner is showing fatigue or frustration: keep the session light and encouraging. Emphasize how much they've learned in 5 sessions. The midpoint is a natural time to celebrate real progress.
- If two or more concepts are still at "introduced" (not yet "practicing"): extend consolidation. Consider running session 7 as another consolidation day before introducing A-05.
